--- ets4 News ---

A newsletter for economic news (financial markets, energy, macroeconomics) and think tank policy 
briefs relevant for forecasting, to be distilled in pills, focused on real time policy and decision 
making.

More RSS Feeds are available among "Policy & Economic Analysis Think Tanks" in
the deprecated pipeline notebooks.

The aim of this newsletter is to collect news and latest forecasts and projections, either in a
qualitative or quantitative form, and to produce a comprehensive overview and summary of the key 
developments useful from the standpoints of a policymaker and planner. To finalize this summary
and provide qualitative predictions and assessments of economic aggregates, energy variables, or 
financial prices, we rely on a large language model (LLM) for reasoning.

Ancestor of 'ets4.py' are the pipelines files in the folder of deprecated scripts.
